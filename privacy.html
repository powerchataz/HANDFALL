Privacy Policy

Handfall — last updated 2026-09-11

Short version: Handfall collects nothing. It has no account system, no analytics, no third-party trackers, no advertising SDK, and makes no network requests to us or anyone else while you play. Everything the game remembers about you stays on your own device.

What the game stores, and where

Handfall saves a small amount of data using your browser's or app's local storage (localStorage), entirely on your own device:

  • Your best score
  • Which achievements you've unlocked
  • Your recent run history (score, best hand, duration, date)
  • Whether you've completed the onboarding screen
  • Your Zen mode, Hard Mode, and mute preferences

None of this data is transmitted anywhere. It never leaves your device, we never see it, and it isn't linked to your identity in any way. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data for Handfall deletes it completely.

Network requests

The only network requests the game itself makes are to load its typeface files from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — standard web font delivery, not analytics or tracking. If you install Handfall as a native app (iOS/ Android), it can run fully offline once those font files are cached.

Third parties

Handfall does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking service of any kind. There is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Handfall does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync or analytics — this page will be updated first, with the "last updated" date above reflecting the change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via an issue on the GitHub repository.
